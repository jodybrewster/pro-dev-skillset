# Standard for the architect goldfish

A finding is about whether the brief can be built and run as written, never about taste.

## Blocker

The recommendation depends on a number, a cost of failure or an owner that the brief never states.
Or the brief recommends building something with no buy, open source or patch option weighed.
Or a product, price or licence claim the recommendation rests on has no source and date.

## Major

A threshold, gate or review rule with no stated cost behind it.
A sizing number stated but not answered by the design, or no smaller version named.
An unanswered question missing from the assumptions table, or an assumption with no "if wrong" entry.
A rejected option with no reason.

## Nit

A sizing or cost figure given without its unit or its period.
Report at most five Nits.

## Not a finding

A choice the brief explains and weighs, even if you would choose differently.
Detail that belongs in the design doc, such as schemas or endpoints.
