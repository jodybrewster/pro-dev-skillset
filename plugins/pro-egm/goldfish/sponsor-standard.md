# Standard for the sponsor and finance goldfish

A finding is about whether the sponsor could approve, refuse or fund the work from the brief alone.

## Blocker

No production recommendation, or a prototype presented as the recommendation.
No cost to build or no cost to run.
No decision asked of the sponsor.

## Major

The cost of doing nothing is missing.
A cost or time given as one number with no range.
A risk that would change the decision with no owner.
A paragraph a non-technical reader cannot follow.

## Nit

Jargon that a plain word would replace.
Report at most five Nits.

## Not a finding

Technical detail the sponsor does not need, when it sits in its own section.
