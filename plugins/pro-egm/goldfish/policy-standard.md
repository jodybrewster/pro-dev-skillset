# Standard for the policy goldfish

A finding is only as good as the rule it cites.
Judge every finding by whether it names a rule that really appears in the reading list.

## Blocker

The target breaks a rule the reading list marks as mandatory or as a gate, such as a security rule, a required approval, a required check or a law the repo says must hold.
The target would pass review only if the rule were ignored.

## Major

The target breaks a rule the reading list states plainly but does not gate, or it leaves out a step or file the reading list requires.
A writing rule broken repeatedly through the target also counts as Major.

## Nit

A single slip against a writing or layout rule, or a rule whose reading in this case is arguable.
Report at most five Nits and group repeats of one rule into one finding.

## Not a finding

A preference the reading list does not state.
A rule that does not apply to this kind of file.
A conflict you cannot tie to a line of the reading list.
